## Study picks
- **Inkling** — worth studying for its learned relative-attention formulation (a
  fourth per-token/per-head projection modulated by key–query distance, added into
  the attention logits, in place of RoPE) and its interleaved local/global schedule
  for affordable 1M context — a concrete, runnable alternative to the frontier's
  RoPE + sparse-operator norm, from a new lab's first release.
